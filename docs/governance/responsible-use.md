# Responsible Use

This project and its outputs must be used responsibly.

## Core statements

- The model is educational.
- It is not reliable for factual answers.
- It may memorize training data.
- It may produce offensive, biased, or nonsensical text.
- It has not undergone alignment training.
- It has not undergone production safety testing.
- It must not be treated as an authority.
- It must not be used for high-stakes decisions.
- It does not represent the capability, scale, or safeguards of a production LLM.

## Recommended use

- learning how language models work
- small-scale experimentation
- studying tokenization, probabilities, and generation behavior

## Disallowed or strongly discouraged use

- medical, legal, or financial decision support
- deceptive or unsafe content generation
- policy or safety claims that exceed actual evidence

## Communication expectations

Always state the model’s limitations, training scope, and evidence level when sharing results.
